2026 to now

AlQuraishi Lab, Columbia

Do genomic language models learn RNA structure?

I rebuilt Minerva's RNA base-pairing benchmark (Rfam 15.0; 672 families, 6.1M candidate pairs) to test whether the lab's genomic language models, up to 661M parameters, encode RNA secondary structure. The signal sits in a few attention heads, and the default head-weight training was undertrained. Caching per-head attention maps cut each training run from hours to seconds, which let me search 90 to 126 configurations per model and raise the 35-layer model's test AUPRC from 0.457 to 0.486, about 80 times random.

COMS 4705, Columbia

Team lead

Does learned bug triage transfer to new codebases?

Bug triage models are usually trained and tested on the same project. In our pilot, BM25 Recall@10 fell from 0.50 on Firefox to 0.19 on VS Code. I lead a team of three, designed a leakage-free evaluation harness with temporal splits and cross-tracker transfer tests over 100K+ bug reports from 8 open-source projects, and am building a dense duplicate-bug retriever trained with contrastive learning and hard negatives in a custom PyTorch loop. Results are expected in December 2026.

Oct 2026

Columbia AI Alignment Club

Trust issues: what actually breaks up an AI cartel?

In a three-agent market of LLM agents (Qwen 3.5 9B via Ollama), I designed a talk-vs-action control experiment. Greedy offers alone kept prices at the cartel level (52), while silent overproduction crashed them to cost (4) within one round. It became a key figure in our team's paper. I also added three experimental conditions to the team's Python simulation framework, with all existing regression tests passing.

2026 to now

LIINC, Columbia

Cleaning EEG recorded inside an MRI scanner

In this simultaneous EEG-fMRI dataset (255 sessions, 33 subjects, 5 kHz), scanner artifacts were about 20 times larger than brain signal. I implemented gradient artifact removal (average artifact subtraction) from scratch in Python, bringing channel amplitude from about 700 to 1,100 µV down to 30 to 55 µV. Systematic QC also caught that 44% of resting-state epochs were scored as sleep and that one subject was recorded without scanner clock sync.

  • Signal processing
  • Python
  • Quality control

2023 to 2026

Zheng Lab, UW Bioengineering

Segmentation-free phenotyping of brain endothelial junctions

Standard cell segmentation failed on dense 3D confocal images, so drug screens across 300+ biological replicates had no quantitative junction readout. I showed segmentation isn't needed: junction shape can be read from intensity profiles between neighboring nuclei. My pipeline extracts 5 junction features across 182 images, clusters phenotypes with Gaussian mixture models, and ranked 25 compounds by how well they rescued barrier junctions. The work supports a co-first-author manuscript and a bioRxiv preprint.
